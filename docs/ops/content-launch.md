# Content launch starter (post-MVP)

Use after the site is viewable on a free `*.vercel.app` or custom domain.

## Weekly cadence (≈4–6 hours)

- 3–5 short videos: one phrase/day (Chinese → pinyin → English → CTA)
- 1–2 SEO notes: e.g. “Chinese phrases for restaurants”, “hotel Chinese for travelers”
- Optional: one helpful Reddit comment/post (not a hard sell)

## First SEO titles to draft

1. Chinese phrases for the airport
2. Hotel Chinese phrases for travelers
3. Restaurant Chinese: order food without English
4. Taxi and subway Chinese phrases
5. Emergency Chinese phrases you should know

## Success check (90 days)

- Any paid unlock (or clear demo→intent signal)
- Repeat visits to `/learn/*`
- Then consider AdSense + learning mini-games
